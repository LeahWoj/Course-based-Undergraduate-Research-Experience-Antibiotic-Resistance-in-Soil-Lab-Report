# Course-based-Undergraduate-Research-Experience-Antibiotic-Resistance-in-Soil-Lab-Report

An HTML write up of an undergraduate lab focused on culturing bacteria from soil samples and determining antibiotic resistance to tetracycline.

## Participants and Collaborators 

This project was done in collaboration with my lab mates: Grace Gleason '28, Amanda Klewin '27, and Beckham Sonnabend '26, at Lake Forest College. This study was a 15-week long semester lab associated with the course Biol-221: Molecules, Genes, and Cells. Its results are in contribution to the PARE Project run by Tufts University.

A special thank you to all those who work in the biology department at Lake Forest College: especially Zoya Marinova for direct guidance, and William Conrad for creation of many lab procedures.
